Ball Bonanza

2022

I tried really hard to make a complete, fun game with this one. Of course I was not very good at it, but looking back I'm very proud of how it turned out.

How to play

Bomb-Bot shoots red bullets and explodes when killed. Ice-Bot's bullets freeze you on impact.

A & D to roll left and right respectively (also aims Banana Bullet)

S to bounce rapidly

Spacebar to jump

R to shoot bullets

E to shoot Banana Bullet

Arrow keys or mouse to aim

F to toggle aiming with arrow keys or mouse

Candy Drop

2023

Average $100 Fiverr quality game. The game over screen was unfortunately broken when putting it on the website but it looked pretty cool, trust.

How to play

Control the gold candy cracker with the mouse cursor. Yellow candies spawn from the top of the screen. Bounce a candy in the air two times to turn it red. The third bounce will break it and earn you a point.
